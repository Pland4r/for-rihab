Reasons I love you

Reason no. 01

The way you say my name when you are happy about something.

not opinions

Things that are simply true about you

  • Your mind

    You think in a way that makes complicated things sit down and behave. I have watched you untangle in ten minutes what other people circle for a week.

  • Your laugh

    It arrives before you mean it to, and it takes the whole room with it. It is my favourite sound on this earth, and it is not close.

  • Your heart

    You are kind in the unglamorous way — the kind that costs you something and asks for nothing back. Most people only perform that. You just do it, quietly.

  • Your courage

    You have carried things that would have flattened most people, and you did it without going hard or cold. Staying soft after all of that is the bravest thing I know of.

  • Your presence

    Rooms are better when you are in them. That is not flattery, it is an observation other people keep making to me about you.

  • Your effect on me

    You make me want to be a better version of myself — not by asking me to be, just by existing nearby. That is a strange and enormous power.

little letters

Open when…

Tap the one that matches your day.

you are sad

Come here. You do not have to explain it or justify it or be productive about it. Sad days are not you failing, they are just weather. Sit in it, let it pass, and remember that there is someone whose whole day gets better the second your name appears on his screen. I am one message away, always.

you miss me

I miss you too. Probably more, though I know you will argue about that. Close your eyes and pick your favourite day with me — I am thinking about the same one. Distance is just a number of hours between now and the next time I get to look at you properly.

you feel ugly

Then your mirror is broken, because mine works perfectly. You are beautiful in a way that photographs keep failing to explain — and I am not only talking about your face, your eyes, or that smile that ruins me. You are beautiful the way a good song is beautiful. It is not up for debate. I was there. I saw you.

you doubt yourself

You are not behind. You are not too much. You are not failing. You are one of the most capable people I have ever met, and I am not a generous judge. Whatever you are doubting today, I would bet everything I own on you. I already did, in a way.

My Rihab,

I am not good at saying all of this out loud. It comes out smaller than I mean it, so I built it somewhere it could sit properly and stay.

You should know what you have done. Before you, I was fine — fine the way a room with the lights off is fine. Nothing wrong with it, nothing happening in it either. Then you walked in and I could suddenly see the whole place.

I know you are hard on yourself. I know there are days you look in the mirror and count only the things you would change. On those days, come here and read this in my voice: you are not too much, you are not behind, you are not failing. You are the most remarkable person I have ever met.

Whatever happens, whatever we build, wherever we end up — I am not going anywhere. You are my favourite person, my calmest place, and the best decision I never had to think twice about.
