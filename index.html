how we got here

Our story, so far

It took the long way around. I would take the long way again.

  1. Three years ago

    I met you

    I did not know yet that I was meeting the person. You were just there, being completely yourself, and something in me quietly took note of you and never let it go.

  2. Then life interfered

    The years apart

    I left the university for a private school, and just like that we were in different stories. Different places, different days, no way back to each other. Three years of almost. I want you to know that you did not leave my head in any of them.

  3. 18 September 2026

    Finally, us

    After three years of the world getting in the way, we finally became us, and the whole thing made sense at once. Everything before this was just the waiting room.

  4. From here

    Everything else

    Every 3rd of January from now on, and every ordinary Tuesday in between. This page will keep counting the days for us. I intend to give it a very large number to count.

not opinions

Things that are simply true about you

  • Your mind

    You think in a way that makes complicated things sit down and behave. I have watched you untangle in ten minutes what other people circle for a week.

  • Your laugh

    It arrives before you mean it to, and it takes the whole room with it. It is my favourite sound on this earth, and it is not close.

  • Your heart

    You are kind in the unglamorous way — the kind that costs you something and asks for nothing back. Most people only perform that. You just do it, quietly.

  • Your courage

    You have carried things that would have flattened most people, and you did it without going hard or cold. Staying soft after all of that is the bravest thing I know of.

  • Your presence

    Rooms are better when you are in them. That is not flattery, it is an observation other people keep making to me about you.

  • Your effect on me

    You make me want to be a better version of myself — not by asking me to be, just by existing nearby. That is a strange and enormous power.

I miss you too. Probably more, though I know you will argue about that. We already survived three years of missing each other — a few hours is nothing now. Distance is just the number of hours between now and the next time I get to look at you properly.

My Rihab,

I am not good at saying all of this out loud. It comes out smaller than I mean it, so I built it somewhere it could sit properly and stay.

Three years ago I met you, and then life did something stupid. I left the university for the private school, and the distance did the rest. I want you to know that in all that time, through everything that changed, you stayed exactly where you were in my head. I never fully put you down.

And then yesterday happened. After three years of almost, of wrong timing and wrong places, you are mine and I am yours, and I keep checking that it is real. It is real.

I know you are hard on yourself. I know there are days you look in the mirror and count only the things you would change. On those days, come here and read this in my voice: you are not too much, you are not behind, you are not failing. You are the most remarkable person I have ever met.

Whatever happens, whatever we build, wherever we end up — I am not going anywhere. Not after waiting this long. You are my favourite person, my calmest place, and the best thing that ever took the long way to me.
